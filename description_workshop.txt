Community Souls Expansion places Mutant after Nameless Deity because the main developer likes Mutant.
Nameless Deity is a much more cinematic, interesting, and fitting final boss than some guy's powerscaling OC.

I respect CSE and its amazing development team, but this change and the refusal to even make it optional greatly upsets me.

This mod overrides CSE's change to the boss order to put Nameless Deity back in it's rightful place as the final boss.
